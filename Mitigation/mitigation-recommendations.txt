Mitigation Strategies for Open Redirection

Recommended Fixes:
1. Validate redirect parameters strictly
2. Allow only internal URLs (whitelisting)
3. Avoid user-controlled redirects
4. Implement proper Content-Security-Policy
5. Configure Referrer-Policy and Permissions-Policy
6. Follow OWASP secure coding practices

Outcome:
Proper validation and security controls can effectively
prevent Open Redirection vulnerabilities.
